which exactly one (`DriverReadPathAllocatesNoManagedBytes`) is an exact allocation window. Its totals

same repetition proof the gate classes get; it is deliberately not folded into the `totals=` string
because that string drives the per-gate *lump signature* predicate, and a structural class has no
`Actual: <lump>` reading to classify.

(`168|5216|7384|7448`) and stops the work otherwise. Across the 120 runs above there were **no
failures of any kind**, so the acceptance predicate was never exercised. The injected-allocation
discrimination re-proof (in `gate-stability.txt`) is the check that the two new exact windows still
fail when they should: both reported `Actual: 88000` and were green again after restoring.

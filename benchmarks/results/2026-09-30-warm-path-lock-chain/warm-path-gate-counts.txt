## Step 2 — bucketed activity: clock call gone, retention never early

Exact facts (`WarmPathGateTests`, green at `c6f58b7` + Steps 1–2):

- `ActivityBucketClockReadsTheClockExactlyOncePerTickAndNeverOnAWarmResolve`: construction 1 read,
  `Current` 0, `Tick()` exactly +1, `Publish(utc)` 0; with a clock armed to throw after one tick, a

## Step 3 — flow-table warm resolve: the direct-mapped warm cache (landed)

Mechanism: the gated `Dictionary` authority is untouched; `FlowState?[] _warm`
(`BitOperations.RoundUpToPowerOf2(clamp(capacity * 64, 4,096, 262,144))` — 262,144 slots / 2 MB at the
shipped default) is populated at claim, written through on every gated resolve hit, and cleared in
`RemoveCandidateAt` with a `ReferenceEquals` guard immediately before `ReturnState`. Slots are keyed by
`FlowHash.CombineCanonical` (endpoints ordered by `(bits, port)`), and a hit is only served after
`FlowState.TrySnapshot` (the Step 2 seqlock) plus transport-tuple corroboration in either orientation,
so a collision, a torn read or a recycled state is a false miss, never a wrong decision.

### Landed facts (`WarmPathGateTests`, all green on the landed tree)

- `WarmResolveTakesNoFlowTableGateEntries` — 256 warm probes ⇒ 256 hits, **0** `_gate` entries.
- `WarmResolveCompletesWhileFlowTableGateIsHeld` — a concurrent warm probe completes while another
  thread is parked inside `_gate` (dedicated threads, bounded waits).
- `FlowTableWarmResolveAllocatesNoManagedBytes` — 256 probes inside an exactly-zero per-thread window
  (probe batch converges at 0 B, thread identity asserted, exact `Assert.Equal(0, allocated)`, call-count
  backstop) on a populated table.
- `FlowTableCollidingFlowsFallBackToTheGatedPath` — a forced canonical-slot collision: the displaced
  flow's warm probe misses, its gated resolve returns its own decision and warms the slot back; both
  flows keep their own decisions throughout.
- `FlowTableRemovedFlowIsNeverServedFromItsOldSlot` — the sweep clears the slot; the recycled instance's
  snapshot describes the new flow; a re-claim serves the new decision and a newer generation.
- `FlowTableTransportTupleIsUniqueAcrossOrigins` — a second claim of the same transport tuple under
  another origin resolves to the existing state (one state, policy evaluated once) — the invariant the
  tuple-only corroboration rests on.
- `FlowTableRegistryMirrorsCountAcrossChurn` — `Count == LiveStateCountForDiagnostics` across 8
  claim/expire rounds; the cache adds no bookkeeping.
- `WarmCacheHitServesTheValidatedView` — a cache hit's `Key`/`Decision`/`Generation` equal the gated
  authority's for the same key, in both orientations.

### Series: the acceptance ratio is NOT met as written

3 runs, `--duration 63`, `--flows 4096` (7 s windows), warm arm (cache probe + the still-gated wildcard
guard — Step 4 has not landed):

| Run | 1t (res/s) | 2t | 4t | 4t self-normalised ratio | 4t vs recorded baseline | miss rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| after 1 | 6,813,459.0 | 10,248,915.3 | 11,061,680.1 | 0.406 | 0.830 | 0.732 % |
| after 2 | 6,586,236.3 | 10,010,978.5 | 10,566,255.2 | 0.401 | 0.793 | 0.932 % |
| after 3 | 6,703,677.3 | 10,104,898.9 | 10,426,814.1 | 0.389 | 0.782 | 0.910 % |

The 1-thread arm clears its ≥ 3.00 M/s floor; the 4-thread **self-normalised ratio is 0.389–0.406
against the ≥ 0.6 line → the criterion as written fails**. Per-lookup CPU cost: 146.8–151.8 ns at 1
thread, 195.1–199.8 ns at 2, 361.6–383.6 ns at 4.

Decomposition from the companion arms in the same runs (run 1, M res/s 1t/2t/4t): `fake` 7.44/4.39/3.78
(gated resolve, no guard), `real` 3.62/3.23/2.59 (guarded, gated resolve), `warm` 6.81/10.25/11.06.
Hence at one thread: guard ≈ `real − fake` ≈ 142 ns, cache probe ≈ `warm − guard` ≈ **7 ns** (vs ~134 ns
for the gated resolve). The one-thread arm is ~95 % guard, and the four-thread ceiling (~11 M/s) is
identical to the reverted concurrent-index variant's 11.06 M/s — so it is set by what both mechanisms
still shared (the wildcard guard's registry gate, Step 4; plus per-hit activity-store coherence), not by
the cache. Miss rate 0.73–0.93 % is already below the design's ~1.6 % target.

**Consequence for the operator's named levers**: raising the slot cap (524,288 / 4 MB) or adding K-way
probing lowers a miss rate that is not the binding term. The ratio's denominator shed the 134 ns gated
resolve (5.16 → 6.7 M/s one-thread) while the numerator is capped by the guard-limited four-thread
ceiling; the criterion should be re-read, or the guard measured separately (Step 4), before any
slot-shape change.

### Residuals recorded, not fixed

- The dispatcher's **slow** path still reads the pooled instance after the gate is released (the
  pre-existing claim-path ABA); `TryResolve`/`TryClaimResolved` keep their signatures per the design, so
  the fix stays a follow-up.
- Step 7's per-iteration tick is still unwired, so the flow-table clock refreshes only on claims and the
  60 s main sweep and retirement of a stopped flow can be ≈60 s early (bounded; Step 7 lands the tick).
- `FlowTable.WarmSlotCountForDiagnostics` was added beyond the plan's diagnostics list so the collision
  fact can compute the slot mask deterministically.

## Step 5 — TCP redirect: one fold probe, two warm caches, no gate on a warm packet

Landed: `TcpRedirectTable._warmReverse`/`_warmOriginal` (direct-mapped, `RoundUpToPowerOf2(clamp(capacity
* 8, 1_024, 16_384))` slots, validated by the association's get-only `ReverseSourceEndpoint`/
`ReverseDestinationEndpoint` and `OriginalKey`), population in `TryClaim`, write-through on the gated
fallback, and one factored `RemoveUnderGate` used by `TryRemove` and both `RemoveExpired` bodies (it
clears both cache entries with a `ReferenceEquals` guard). `IsReverseCandidate` is deleted; the
coordinator resolves once via `TryResolveByReverse` and passes the association into
`HandleReverseAsync`, whose own probe is gone. `HandlePacketAsync` keeps the listener-port prefilter in
front of the probe. Association activity is a bucket stamp; `TcpRedirectTable.RemoveExpired` and
`TcpRedirectSessionStore.RemoveExpiredAsync` compare `BucketForDiagnostics < ActivityBucket.Cutoff(now,
idleTimeout)`. `Count` still reads `_byOriginal.Count` under `_gate` — **no maintained counter** (the
plan's `_count` line is superseded by design §5 / review disposition 16: the `Dictionary` authority is
unchanged, so a counter would be a second source of truth).

Diagnostics: `TcpRedirectTable.ReverseProbeCountForDiagnostics` was added (counted under the same
attachment as `GateEntryCountForDiagnostics`) because the fold's contract is a **probe** count, not a
gate count.

## Step 6 — UDP ready path: ready-first, wait-free, no session gate

Landed: `TrySendSpanAsync` probes a pre-allocated session cache (`UdpSessionSlot?[]`,
`clamp(capacity * 8, 1_024, 16_384)` slots) and validates `slot.Session.Flow`; everything else takes the
admission path (`SendAdmissionPathSpanAsync`), which keeps the cooldown probe, the clock read, the
capacity check and the under-gate `slot.Ready` re-check. `_sessions` stays the gated `Dictionary`
authority; the cache is published at the admission add and at the ready flip and cleared with a
`ReferenceEquals` guard on removal (`Array.Clear` on the dispose drain, where every slot goes).
`UdpSessionSlot.Session`/`Ready` are volatile accessors with `Session` published first.
`UdpProxySession.SendSpanAsync`/`TouchActivity` drop `_activityGate` (scope CAS only); the activity
stamp is the published bucket with a `long.MinValue` "never propagated" sentinel; `TryBeginExpiry`
re-checks `ActivityBucket.Cutoff` under the gate.

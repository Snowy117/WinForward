# Step 6 verdict: the single-pass IPv6 address delta is rejected by measurement

Task `09-30-flow-key-parse-once`. `implement.md` Step 6 is an **optional commit**: the single-pass,
wide-load IPv6 address delta in `PacketChecksums.TryRewriteIpv6Tcp`, adopted "only if the post-Step-5
re-run shows a win on the IPv6 reverse rows with every other row unmoved" (`design.md` §6). It was
**not adopted**. This file records the readings the decision is made on; nothing here is a gate.

**Every one of the 16 rows improves**, so AC-4's discharge — per-leg no-regression — holds with no

Two readings that point the other way, both recorded rather than smoothed over:

- **The IPv6:IPv4 ratio widened**: 1400 B 1.65 → **1.92**; 128 B 2.08 → **2.29**. The work the
  landing steps removed (a lock pair, three header walks) is family-neutral, while the remaining
  family-specific term is the IPv6 checksum delta, which shrinks neither side proportionally.
- **The absolute IPv6−IPv4 gap narrowed**: 1400 B 48.07 → **34.97 ns** (−13.1); 128 B 58.19 →
  **33.65 ns** (−24.5). The row's family-specific residual is real and smaller than it was.

## Why the optional commit is dropped

1. **Its precondition is false.** `design.md` §6 adopts the delta only when "the IPv6 reverse rows
   still read slow **with every other row unmoved**". Every other row moved — and improved — so this
   series cannot attribute anything to a checksum-shaped change. The recorded 1.65 → 1.92 ratio is a
   *reading*, and the PRD restated AC-4 around exactly that: per-leg no-regression is the discharge,
   the improvement and the ratio are readings, not gates.
2. **The only measured evidence for the change is below this host's noise floor.** The planning probe
   measured the wide-load delta at **22.1–22.6 ns vs 27.6–28.3 ns** narrow (`research/
   implementation-notes.md` §7.3) — ≈5.5 ns on the isolated delta, ≈7.5 % of the 72.87 ns AC-4 row,
   and `hot-path.md` contract 9 makes sub-2× deltas on this host unmeasurable. Its bit-identity proof
   (2,000,000 random 32-byte pairs, 0 mismatches) is an equivalence proof, not a win.
3. **The larger structural half is unmeasured.** Dropping the 32-byte `stackalloc` snapshot and its
   second pass is the bigger term (the rewriter's family delta measured +43 to +49 ns: IPv6
   63.7–69.8 vs IPv4 20.3–20.8), and it cannot be separated from host noise without editing product
   code. Landing an unmeasured rewrite of the transparent-redirect primitive
   (`quality-guidelines.md:14`) is the opposite of the evidence-gated discipline this task ran on.

Re-opening the step would need a controlled A/B of the delta rewrite itself with the host quiet on
all four reverse-forwarded cells; that measurement does not exist and the landing steps already
delivered the row's improvement, so the option is closed as **rejected by measurement**.

## Attribution (which step owns the AC-4 row)

`tcp-redirect-data-path` is `memcpy + TrackServerSequence + TryRewriteTcpEndpoints` — it contains no
key and no dictionary probe (`research/implementation-notes.md` §7.2), so the key steps must **not**
be credited here. The named mechanisms are:

- **Step 2** (atomic CAS-max trackers): removes the `_sequenceGate` lock pair from
  `TrackServerSequence` on **both** legs, measured 22.9–29.0 ns uncontended at census time. Step 1
  recorded the red count (**2** gate entries per packet pair), Step 2 the after count (**0**).
- **Step 3** (parse once): the row's re-parsing sequence observation and its span-revalidating
  rewriter became the layout-driven entries (walk counts 4/4 → 1/1 per leg).
- Steps 4–5 (packed key) and Step 7 (slim context) are **not** on this row and are not credited with
  its movement; they are measured on `flow-table-production-shape` and `dispatcher` respectively.
